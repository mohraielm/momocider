Extract track metadata to infuse your music in MP3 form! A bridge between physical and digital media!
Momocider is a nostalgic, cozy utility designed to bridge the gap between digital playlists and physical collections. It simplifies the creation of physical MP3 Audio CDs by taking digital inputs (YouTube URLs), automatically extracting essential metadata, and structuring the data for easy installation onto blank media.

🛠️ The Process Broken Down
We’ve curated a simple, four-step ritual to transform your digital vibes into lasting physical mixtapes.

### Step 1: 📡 The Gathering
**Goal:** Collect your digital raw ingredients.
Your mixtape journey begins in the vast orchard of the internet.

*   **Action:** Find the songs you want on your mixtape (e.g., YouTube videos) and collect their URLs.
*   **In Momocider:** Paste the URL(s) into the momocider track input. Momocider will automatically communicate with the backend to retrieve initial track details.

### Step 2: 🍃 The Infusion
**Goal:** Extract and infuse the track details.
Raw data needs flavor and context. This is where we distill the essential properties of each track.

*   **Action:** Momocider fetches and organizes key information for your tracks.
*   **In Momocider:**
    *   The backend pulls full track metadata: Title, Artist, and Duration (Seconds).
    *   It also snags the Thumbnail URL for a visual reference in your playlist.
    *   Your mohraielm/momocider frontend updates dynamically, filling in the details and adjusting the 80-minute CD Capacity Meter in real-time.

### Step 3: 💿 The Pressing
**Goal:** Solidify your music as an MP3 audio CD.
Your ingredients are infused and ready. We must now prepare them for physical storage.

*   **Action:** Solidify the playlist data structure into a physical format.
*   **In Momocider:** When your mixtape is complete and the capacity is optimized, Momocider generates an optimized output. While the final burn is up to you (e.g., using your OS’s disc burner), Momocider has organized everything, ensuring correct track order and optimized space utilization for a standard 700MB / 80-minute CD.

### Step 4: 🌉 The Bridge
**Goal:** Walk the bridge from physical to digital.
Your physical creation is complete. You may now enjoy your digital collection on any physical player.

*   **Action:** Take your freshly burned MP3 CD and insert it into any compatible CD player (car stereo, boombox, hi-fi system).
*   **Outcome:** The physical media holds the digital MP3 files you collected and infused. The journey is complete: you have crossed the bridge, installing your digital music back into the analog world for shared, tangible listening.
